One family of programs for your laser, CNC mill and pen plotter

Design or import a drawing, give every layer a job, look at the result in the preview and send G-code to the machine. The same window, the same tools and the same shortcuts in all the programs. No machine? BaseCreator is the same editor for drawing alone.

LightCreator

Laser engravers and cutters

v0.8.3

An open-source program for laser engravers and cutters. Cut layers, line, fill and image engraving with six dithering methods, a camera overlay, a toolpath preview with the time of the job and a G-code viewer.

  • GRBL (0.7 to 1.1 and newer) and Marlin over a serial port or the network
  • Ruida / Trocen jobs as PLT / DXF
  • Tested on a Sculpfun S9 diode laser
Download Source on GitHub

MillCreator

CNC milling machines

v0.8.3

Layers with an operation (outside, inside, on the line, pocket, drill) and a bit from the library, a 3D preview of the material, picture engraving and 3D relief, streaming to GRBL (0.7 to 1.1 and newer) with probing and macros.

  • Bit library with seven kinds of bits
  • 3D view and an animated simulation
  • Tested on a TwoTrees TTC450Pro
Download Source on GitHub

PenCreator

Pen plotters

v0.8.3

Pens as layers: colour, width, speed, outline and fill lines. A plot preview with the pen-up moves, the number of lifts and the time, stroke ordering to shorten the moves, and a pause to change the pen.

  • Z axis or a servo pen lift, GRBL 0.7 to 1.1 and newer, AxiDraw / EggBot and HPGL plotters
  • Pen library with the mark each pen makes
  • Pictures drawn with dots or hatching
  • Tested on a pen plotter built after the Drawing Robot (Arduino UNO, CNC Shield, GRBL)
Download Source on GitHub

BaseCreator

Drawing, with no machine

Drawing only v1.82.1

The same editor without devices, G-code or a camera, with a page in place of a work area. It writes the same project files, so a drawing made here can be finished for a machine in the other programs.

  • A page of your own: A5 to A1, Letter, Legal or any size
  • The same import and export lists as the other programs
  • Plugins that make shapes and read files work here too
Download Source on GitHub

Built together, on baseCreator

All four programs share one core, so a new tool or fix lands in every one of them.

30 languages

Polish, English, German, Spanish, Chinese, Hindi, Greek and more. Switch in the start window.

Windows, macOS, Linux

Installers for Windows, .dmg for Apple silicon and Intel, .deb and .tar.gz for x86_64 and ARM64.

Free and open

MIT or Apache-2.0. No account, no subscription, no telemetry.

The same files everywhere

Every program reads and writes the files of LightBurn and MillMage (.lbrn2, .lbrn, .mage), DXF (current and R12), Illustrator and SVG with pictures; it also imports PDF, bitmaps with tracing and UGS Designer designs, and exports PLT.

A shared clipart gallery

310 pictures in 9 categories, your own pictures and online search of open icon collections.

One project file

A .cre project keeps one drawing with a section for every program, so you can laser a part and mill another.

A G-code viewer

Open any G-code file from the start window, without a project: flat or in 3D, with travel moves, colour by height, size and time.

A navigation cube

Click a face, an edge or a corner of the cube in a 3D view to look from there, the same as in FreeCAD.

Control the machine

Jogging, probing, start from a line, macros, the firmware settings and calibration, over a serial port or the network.

Tabs of drawings

Open up to 10 drawings at once, each in its own tab. Ctrl+1…9 switches, Ctrl+W closes, and on exit every unsaved tab is asked about, each with its own recovery file.

The same Control tab

Home, Unlock, Pause and Resume, START and STOP, jogging and speed look and work the same in the laser, the mill and the plotter.

Trace the work first

Go round the job before you start it: the box in every program, and in LightCreator also the line that wraps all the shapes, like a rubber band.

One element at a time

Burn, cut or draw each element completely, all its layers and passes, before the next one, or only what you have selected: two switches in the Control tab.

The camera beside your drawing

The live camera view (also an RTSP stream) as a tab next to your drawings, fitted to the window, or in a window of its own, with photos, video and a time-lapse every N seconds or after each layer, into a folder you choose.

Pen change at a place of the machine

PenCreator drives to the place where the pen is changed, runs the G-code of your mechanism and stops there.

The milled piece in 3D

MillCreator shows the material as it will be milled, with the real shape of every bit (on a cylinder when a rotary attachment is chosen), and saves it as an STL model. Own G-code and a work coordinate system (G54 to G59) are in the device settings.

Preview as a tab

Pin the preview next to your drawings, in every program, or let it float. Plugins with settings, like the G-code tools, get a tab of their own.

Levels for pictures

A histogram with a black and a white point when you import or adjust a picture, and a trace dialog that zooms in on the outlines.

Print the drawing

Print it on a paper printer, to scale or as large as the page allows, to check the placement or use it as a template. Or save it as a PDF.

A quick guide

Help → Quick guide, or F1, explains the program step by step, in English and Polish.

A picture under the drawing

Put a photo under the drawing and line it up with the material. LightCreator, MillCreator and PenCreator can also show the live picture of a camera. The project keeps the picture.

USB keyboards and pads

Drive the machine with a pad or the keyboard: moves while you hold a key, Stop, Home, and the spindle, air, water, laser beam or pen, on the keys you choose. One device can serve many machines, and only the window in front listens.

The project on the material

In MillCreator's 3D view the shapes of the drawing can lie on top of the material, in the colours of their layers, so you can compare them with what the bit will cut.

Superman, Batman and Joker

Three new colour schemes for the window, with Joker in a dark and a light version, 18 in all.

AxiDraw, EggBot and HPGL plotters

PenCreator also drives plotters that do not speak G-code: the AxiDraw and the EggBot (EiBotBoard) and HPGL plotters on a serial port.

Paste a picture

Ctrl+V with a screenshot or a picture copied from a browser opens the import window, with its levels, zoom and size.

Keys for the nodes

In node editing D deletes, I inserts, L and S make a line or a curve, C a corner, R rounds and B breaks; a node dropped onto another joins them.

Cut with a drag knife

PenCreator and MillCreator cut vinyl, paper, card and rubber with a drag knife: the offset of the tip is made good at every corner, the holes are cut first and every closed outline closes.

A knife library

Ready-made 30°, 45° and 60° blades and a drag knife in a holder of a CNC machine, and knives of your own with their offset, corners, overcut and notes, kept with your settings.

HPGL cutters their own way

Every HPGL plotter or cutter gets its own commands, on a page of the device that only such devices have: the start and the end, knife down and up, the speed with a top limit and the force.

Plugins

Install generators, importers and G-code postprocessors from the official list with one click: Settings → Plugins… → Official plugins. Each one runs in a sandbox and works in every program that can use it.

See all plugins
  • Circuit boards from KiCad, Gerber, EAGLE and EasyEDA
  • Boxes with finger joints for a laser
  • Nets of boxes to cut out of card
  • Fractals, gears, holes on a circle
  • Barcodes, x,y point files
  • Postprocessors: line numbers, Mach3 / Mach4 / UCCNC, LinuxCNC

Or with a package manager

macOS · Homebrewbrew install --cask michalsarna/tap/lightcreator
Windows · Scoopscoop bucket add creator https://github.com/michalsarna/scoop-bucket
Windows · Scoopscoop install lightcreator

Use millcreator, pencreator or basecreator in place of lightcreator for the other programs. The same commands update to every new release. winget (MichalSarna.LightCreator and the others) follows once Microsoft has accepted the packages.

macOS: the apps are not notarized yet, so open them the first time with right-click → Open. Check downloads with SHA256SUMS.txt. You can also build every program from source with Rust.

⚠️ A laser and a milling machine are dangerous. Try every new job in the air first and never leave a running machine alone.
